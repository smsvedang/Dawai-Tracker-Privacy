Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 | Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Welcome to DawaiTracker ("we", "our", or "us"). We value the privacy and personal trust of our users. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, protected, and handled when you use the DawaiTracker mobile application (the "App").

🔒 Our Privacy Commitment DawaiTracker is designed exclusively as a personal medication reminder and family caregiver adherence assistant. We do NOT sell your personal or health data to advertisers, pharmaceutical companies, or third-party data brokers.

1. Compliance with Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act

This privacy policy is framed in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. Information We Collect

To provide medication reminders and family caregiver syncing, we collect the following minimum necessary data:

  • Account Authentication: Mobile phone number (used for passwordless SMS OTP login via Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile Information: User display name and role selection (e.g., Patient, Caregiver, or Family Member).
  • Medication Schedules: Medicine names, dosage amounts, scheduled reminder timings, frequency, and instructions (e.g., before/after meals).
  • Proof-of-Intake Photos: Photos captured by the patient to verify medication intake.
  • Audio Voice Prompts: Voice recordings recorded by caregivers or patients in local languages for personalized audio reminder alerts.
  • Prescription Documents: Images or documents of doctor prescriptions uploaded voluntarily by the user for safekeeping.
  • Family Linking Data: Unique 6-digit family linking codes to connect caregivers with patient accounts.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

The app requests the following Android system permissions for core functionality:

  • Exact Alarm (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `USE_EXACT_ALARM`): Essential to trigger critical medication reminder alarms at the exact scheduled minute, even when the phone is in battery-saving sleep mode.
  • Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): To show reminder banners and missed-dose alerts.
  • Camera (`CAMERA`): To capture photo proof when taking medicine or to photograph prescriptions.
  • Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`): To record personalized voice prompts in your mother tongue.
  • Storage / Media: To select existing medicine photos or prescription documents from your gallery.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Trigger timely alarms and visual reminders for your scheduled medicines.
  • Allow authorized family caregivers to view daily adherence status and intake photos.
  • Send notifications when a critical dose is overdue.
  • Safely back up your prescriptions and reminders so they are available across your devices.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers to run the application:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore): For secure authentication and encrypted cloud database storage.
  • Cloudinary: For secure, encrypted media storage (intake photos, voice prompts, prescription files).

All third-party processors are bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.

6. Data Security

We implement stringent technical and organizational security measures:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Access to patient records is strictly restricted to authenticated users and explicitly linked family caregivers.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion

Under the DPDP Act and store guidelines, you retain complete control over your personal data:

  • Access & Correction: You can edit your profile, medicines, and prescriptions directly inside the app at any time.
  • Instant Account & Data Deletion: You can permanently wipe your account and all associated data by navigating to Settings ➔ Delete Account within the app. Upon confirmation, your authentication record, medicine schedules, intake logs, and uploaded photos/audio files are immediately and irrevocably deleted.

8. Children's Privacy

DawaiTracker is intended for individuals aged 18 and older. Minors may use the app under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian who manages their medication schedules.
